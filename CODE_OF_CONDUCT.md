# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome questions and different
experience levels, criticize ideas constructively, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
doxxing and knowingly publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and project community
spaces, including conduct elsewhere that directly affects this community.

Maintainers may edit or remove offending content and warn, restrict or exclude
participants. Enforcement should consider context and severity, explain the
decision when appropriate, and protect reporters' privacy.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer through the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Gopher0727) and request a private channel.
Do not include sensitive personal details in public issues. Reports are considered
in good faith; retaliation against a reporter is unacceptable.
